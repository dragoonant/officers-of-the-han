# Officers of the Han

An original strategy game for the browser, set in China's Three Kingdoms era. You play **one officer** in a living world of about 200 historical officers, starting in 190 as the eastern lords rise against Dong Zhuo. Work your city, travel, make friends, swear oaths and marry, take missions from your lord or defy him, duel and debate, fight hex-field sieges, and rise from wanderer to ruler.

**Unofficial, non-commercial fan project by evilgouki.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Koei Tecmo. A spiritual successor to the officer-play Three Kingdoms strategy games; the rules, names of systems, text, art and music are original. Historical people and events come from the public-domain *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* and the historical record.
