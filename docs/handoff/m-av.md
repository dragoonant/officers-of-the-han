# m-av: the art, music and sound pass, built up to the generation step (2026-11-07)

Branch `m-av` (a separate git worktree). Decision D20 in `PLAN.md`; specs `docs/spec/55-portraits.md` and `docs/spec/60-audio.md`; the PC session follows `docs/handoff/m5-av-runbook.md`.

## What was built
- **Specs:** `55-portraits.md` (style line, per-officer prompt rules, crop and output, review process, runtime resolution), `60-audio.md` (cue list, mood map, effect triggers, loudness, 5 MB budget, settings, runner), a pointer section in `50-presentation.md`, D20 and five rulings in PLAN.
- **Portrait pipeline** `tools/gen/portraits/`: `build-prompts.mjs` writes `portrait-prompts.json` (195 officers from `src/data` plus 96 generic `face-NN`, fixed FNV seed per id, lint for rule 23 words, game names and officer names); `gen-portraits.mjs` (Flux Studio, `model:'klein'`, 2 candidates, one job at a time, resumable, waits while busy, `freeAfter:false` then restored, stops after 3 failures in a row, cancels on Ctrl-C); `contact-sheets.mjs` (4×4 labelled PNG sheets, pure Node plus ffmpeg decode, own 5×7 font); `pick-portraits.mjs` (square crop on the face centre, 256 and 128 px WebP, manifest, picks file); `fake-flux-studio.mjs`. npm scripts `portraits:prompts|gen|sheets|pick|fake`.
- **Game side of portraits:** `Portrait` prefers `public/portraits/gen/<id>.webp` when `gen/manifest.json` lists the officer id or the created officer's `face-NN`, falls back to the older file, then the medallion (`portraitSource.ts`, `usePortraitManifest.ts`). The world seam passes `id` (Hud, NewGame, Views). The create-officer picker lists generated faces first.
- **Audio pipeline** `tools/audio/` (ported from the sibling game, new cue tables): `music-cues.json` (13 music cues, 18 effects, instruments-and-mechanism prompts, exclusions in every prompt, "no music, no voices" in every effect), `gen-music.mjs`, `master-audio.mjs` (adds a `sfx` role, OGG q4, optional `--alt` M4A, shipped-size report and budget warning, per-cue `minCrestDb`), `fake-audio-studio.mjs`, `lib.mjs`, `dsp.mjs`. Audition, `--pick`, `--prune`, the report and its targets are kept. npm scripts `audio:gen|master|fake`.
- **In-game audio** `src/client/audio/`: engine, manifest, lazy graph, director (beds with 2 s crossfades, the charge layer phase-locked to the battle bed, rate-limited one-shot cues), mood map, scene flags, game binding, hooks, settings panel. Settings (music, effects, mute) in `localStorage` `oth-audio`, in the Menu and a "Sound" slip on the title screen. Hooks: button taps, city and officer selection, letters and council stings, month end, battle moves and attacks, tactics, duel rounds, debate tablets and results, battle and end jingles. `public/audio/manifest.json` is committed empty; with no files nothing plays, nothing errors.
- **`sounds.html`** (third Vite page): every cue slot with Play/Stop or "no file yet", mood tester and charge button, and the state of all 291 portraits.
- **Edits to shared client files (small):** `App.tsx` (bind, slip), `Game.tsx` (panel in the Menu), `BattleScreen.tsx`, `DuelScreen.tsx`, `DebateScreen.tsx` (scene flag and hooks), `battle/store.ts` (one line in the director's `start`), `world/seam.tsx`, `Hud.tsx`, `NewGame.tsx`, `Views.tsx` (portrait id), `ui/Portrait.tsx`, `vite.config.ts` (sounds page). Not touched: `MapView.tsx`, `geo.ts`, `world.css`, `theme.css`; the CSS is in `src/client/audio/audio.css` and `src/sounds/sounds.css`.

## What is tested, and how
- `tests/unit/portrait-pipeline.test.ts` (16): prompt coverage and the committed file equal to the builder's output, lint (rule 23, game names, officer names), temperament and role clauses, age at appearance, sourced traits only, generic variety, crop maths, and the real runner, contact sheets and pick against the fake Flux Studio (two candidates, one job at a time, klein, fixed seeds, resume with zero requests, `--limit`, waiting while busy, dry run, unreachable studio, a failed image, 256 and 128 px WebP with the right colour, manifest merge).
- `tests/unit/portrait-source.test.ts` (5): the resolution order and the 128 or 256 choice.
- `tests/unit/audio-runner.test.ts` (39): the cue table against the spec (tempo, key, bars, loop lengths, phase-lock pair, studio limits, budget estimate, prompt lint), bar maths, signal analysis, command lines, manifest building, and the pipeline against the fake Audio Studio on a six-cue subset (dry run, generation, resume, force and archive, waiting for another job, unreachable studio, ffmpeg missing, mastering lengths to the sample, loudness within 1 LU, bar 1 at 0.000 s, `--alt`, `--audition`, `--pick --prune`).
- `tests/unit/audio-director.test.ts` (36), `audio-support.test.ts` (17): director ramps, crossfade, fallbacks, silence, phase-lock, chains, failsafe, cue gaps and caps; manifest validation, settings, mood map, `warNear`, battle and duel cue maps.
- `tests/e2e/audio.spec.ts` (4): with no files, title, `sounds.html` and the lab load with 0 console errors and 0 failed requests; the Menu panel changes volumes and mute, persists in `oth-audio`, survives a reload and Continue, and a few months play silently; with a route-faked manifest and a generated WAV the director plays the title bed after the first gesture (the real `AudioContext` in Chromium); with a faked portrait manifest the generated portrait wins and others fall back.

## What is not verified (all real generation)
- No portrait, no music, no effect has been generated. Nothing was run against the real Flux Studio or Audio Studio: the job status words, the file names, the `1:1` aspect, whether `batch` and `seed` behave as the KB says, and `POST /api/settings` are read from the KB only (the readers are lenient and tested against fakes).
- The prompts are **not verified by eye or ear**: whether klein produces the intended mineral-pigment look, whether the role and temperament clauses read right, whether ACE-Step holds the BPM and key, whether Stable Audio gives clean one-shots without music.
- The GPU times and the 5.2 MB size estimate are estimates from KB numbers. The 6 MB portrait size is an estimate.
- Loop seams, the charge layer's lock, the mood crossfades and every effect timing were checked by machine only (fake clock, one real `AudioContext` bed).
